# How Kawkit works

Kawkit turns weekly marketing spend and revenue into a budget recommendation, with every estimate shown as a range rather than a single number. It takes three steps and a few minutes.

## 1. Upload your data

Upload a CSV with one row per week: a date, revenue, and one spend column per channel. If anything is wrong with the file, the problems are shown straight away with row numbers.

## 2. Add what you know

Optionally tell the model what you already know, such as an ROI range from a lift test or how long an effect lasts. Leave it blank to let the data lead.

## 3. Get ranges, not guesses

Kawkit fits a Bayesian model and returns:

- each channel's return, with a 90% range;
- a recommended budget split, with the probability that it beats your current one.

## Try it

The sample result needs no account. It includes a made-up example with known answers and five real e-commerce brands.
